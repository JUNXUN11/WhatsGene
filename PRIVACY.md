# WhatsGene Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. WhatsGene is a research and education tool. It is not medical advice.

## What we collect

- The sequence, FASTA text, or accession you submit, and your plain-language question.
- Search records needed to show and explain your results: BLAST program, database, exact parameters (E-value threshold, word size, matrix), parsed hits (accession, organism, identity, coverage, bit score, E-value), classifications, verification outcomes, and the agent step log.
- Your WhatsApp number, only when you start a job from WhatsApp or give a number on the website for the completion notification. It is stored server-side as your job-history key and is never shown on results pages.

## How it is used

- Local searches run against a database on our server; nothing is sent to NCBI in that mode. Remote searches (only when that backend is selected) send your sequence to NCBI's public BLAST service, which has its own privacy practices.
- Results are used to answer your follow-up questions from stored values only, and to send you the completion notification on WhatsApp.

## Retention and deletion

- Job history is kept so follow-up questions can be answered from stored results.
- Delete it at any time: message WhatsGene on WhatsApp with "forget my history", and your stored jobs for that number are deleted.
